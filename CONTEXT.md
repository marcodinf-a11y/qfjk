# qfjk

qfjk is a virtual tabletop for a GM and players sharing a role-playing session. It starts with D&D 5e 2014 and allows homebrew and future rulesets.

## Language

**Game Master (GM)**:
The participant who prepares and adjudicates play and holds authority over the shared session.

**Player**:
A participant who plays a character under the GM's adjudication.

**Character sheet**:
The presentation of a character's rules-relevant statistics, abilities, possessions, and current state.

**Homebrew rule**:
A rule adopted by a group that adds to or overrides its chosen published rules. The group's natural-1 critical-failure rule for skill checks is one example.

**Automatic resolution**:
Applying the configured rules to resolve an action without requiring the GM to approve every result. It is optional and can differ among creatures in the same encounter.

**Player map view**:
The map information displayed to a player, which may include shared party information and previously explored terrain. Its contents do not by themselves establish what that player's character can currently perceive.

**Character perception**:
What a character can currently perceive given its position, senses, conditions, and surroundings. It informs rules that require perception, including targeting and reaction eligibility.

**Exploration history**:
The party's record of discovered terrain, retained when it is no longer currently visible. It does not reveal current enemy activity in unseen areas.

**Party inventory**:
The group's shared possessions and money, distinct from an individual character's inventory. Items and money can move between these inventories.
